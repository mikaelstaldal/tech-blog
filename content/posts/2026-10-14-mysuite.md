As I have [written before](/tech/2025/07/12/simple-webapp-with-go/), you can build efficient and easy to host web apps
with [Go](https://go.dev/) and [SQLite](https://sqlite.org/).

I have now developed a suite of personal productivity apps with Go, SQLite and 
[TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)+[Preact](https://preactjs.com/) for the web UI.

* Calendar: [MyCal](https://github.com/mikaelstaldal/mycal)
* Mail: [MyMail](https://github.com/mikaelstaldal/mymail)
* Notes: [MyNotes](https://github.com/mikaelstaldal/mynotes)

And a simple app to save links with Go, SQLite and [htmx](https://htmx.org/):

* [MyLinks](https://github.com/mikaelstaldal/mylinks)

I call the whole suite [MySuite](https://mikaelstaldal.github.io/mysuite/), which also contains a password manager 
[MyPass](https://github.com/mikaelstaldal/mypass) made in [Rust](https://rust-lang.org/).

Each of those five has its own native Android app (made in [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)).

All apps are fully self-hosted, so you are in complete control over your data. The ultimate form of 
[digital sovereignty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_sovereignty).
